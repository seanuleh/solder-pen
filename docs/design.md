OpenSCAD file in [`parts/`](../parts/); multi-part prints get a folder in [`stl/`](../stl/) with a
ready-to-slice 3MF (exported without colours, so Bambu Studio keeps the AMS filaments).

## Printing

PETG, no supports, as modelled (each file's header lists the print orientation; the 3MF has every part laid
out in its print orientation). Bambu PETG HF on an X1C, 0.16 mm Optimal. v1 was printed in SUNLU PETG, which
strings badly; not used since.

## Commands

```sh
./checks/run.sh        # one solid per part, plate part counts, no ceilings, assembly paths clear (must all PASS)
./stl/export.sh        # all STLs + 3MFs (or: ./stl/export.sh solder_pen_rig)
./renders/render.sh    # every PNG (or: ./renders/render.sh hero rig_step3); renders/solder_pen_anim.sh = concept A video
python3 docs/diagrams/make.py   # regenerate the how-it-works SVGs, then ./renders/render.sh how_it_works pinch
```

Conventions: `renders/render.sh` holds every camera; the assembly steps and the hero are trimmed by
`renders/trim.py`. **After changing geometry or a camera, look at every changed PNG.** The diagrams take their
numbers from `docs/diagrams/make.py`, which copies them from `parts/solder_pen_rig.scad`: change both.
